How far is Abishek Hospital from Namakkal?

Abishek Hospital is at No.18, Near Bus Stand, Bharathi Street, Erode Fort, Erode – 638001, close to the bus stand. From Namakkal it is about 57 km by road, usually about 1 to 1½ hours.

Namakkal and Erode are linked by road, and buses connect the two towns. There are also train routes through Salem or Karur, so check the timetable before you plan.

Planning your visit from Namakkal

A few things make a longer journey easier:

  • Plan your route the day before. Bus and train timings change, and it helps to know your return option.
  • Book first. Message us on WhatsApp or call 97505 11225 so your visit is confirmed. Clinic hours are Mon – Sat, 9:00 AM – 6:30 PM.
  • Bring your papers. Earlier scan and lab reports, prescriptions, a list of your medicines and your antenatal card if you are pregnant.
  • Ask about your schedule. If you are pregnant, ask the doctor at your first visit how often to come, and when it is wise to stay closer to the hospital near your due date.

Pregnant and far from home? Know when not to wait.

Call 97505 11225 or go to the nearest hospital straight away for bleeding, leaking of water, much less baby movement, regular painful contractions, a severe headache or blurred vision, severe tummy pain, or a fit. Emergency care at Abishek Hospital is available 24 hours a day. In a life-threatening emergency dial 108 or 112 for an ambulance.

Frequently asked questions

Is Abishek Hospital in Namakkal?
No. Our hospital is at No.18, Near Bus Stand, Bharathi Street, Erode Fort, Erode – 638001, about 57 km from Namakkal. We do not have a branch in Namakkal.
How do I get from Namakkal to Abishek Hospital?
Namakkal and Erode are linked by road, and buses connect the two towns. There are also train routes through Salem or Karur, so check the timetable before you plan. For the exact route, get directions on Google Maps.
How do I get from Namakkal to Abishek Hospital?
Most women go by bus or car. By road it is about 57 km. Use the “Get directions” button on our contact page to open the route in Google Maps.
Is emergency care available at night for women from Namakkal?
Yes. Emergency care is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Call 97505 11225 before you set out so the team is ready. In a life-threatening emergency dial 108 or 112 for an ambulance.
Which treatments can I get at Abishek Hospital?
Pregnancy care and delivery, normal and painless (epidural) delivery, high-risk pregnancy care, gynaecology and lady doctor consultations, PCOS and PCOD treatment, fertility and IUI treatment, laparoscopic surgery, menopause care and family planning. See all services and how to reach us.
