One hospital, easy to reach from many towns

Abishek Hospital is a women’s health and maternity hospital at No.18, Near Bus Stand, Bharathi Street, Erode Fort, Erode – 638001, close to the bus stand. We have a single hospital and no branches in other cities. Erode is a busy rail and road junction in western Tamil Nadu, which is why women from nearby towns can reach us in an hour or two.

Under Dr. Jayanthi Prabakaran, we provide pregnancy care and delivery, normal and painless delivery, high-risk pregnancy care, gynaecology, PCOS treatment, fertility and IUI treatment and laparoscopic surgery. Emergency care is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

How to reach Erode Fort

By train

Erode Junction is a major railway junction. Lines run from Erode towards Coimbatore and Tiruppur, towards Salem, and towards Karur and Tiruchirappalli, and many trains connect Erode with Chennai (roughly six to seven hours). Check the IRCTC or railway timetable before you travel. From the station, an auto or taxi takes you to Erode Fort.

By bus or car

The hospital is at No.18, Near Bus Stand, Bharathi Street, Erode Fort, close to the bus stand. Buses connect Erode with the surrounding towns. Use the map link below for the route from where you are.

Planning a visit from another town

A few things make a longer journey easier:

  • Book first. Message us on WhatsApp or call 97505 11225 so your visit is confirmed. Clinic hours are Mon – Sat, 9:00 AM – 6:30 PM.
  • Bring your papers. Earlier scan and lab reports, prescriptions, a list of your medicines and your antenatal card if you are pregnant.
  • Ask about your schedule. If you are pregnant, ask the doctor at your first visit how often to come, and when it is wise to stay closer to the hospital near your due date.

Pregnant and far from home? Know when not to wait.

Call 97505 11225 or go to the nearest hospital straight away for bleeding, leaking of water, much less baby movement, regular painful contractions, a severe headache or blurred vision, severe tummy pain, or a fit. Emergency care at Abishek Hospital is available 24 hours a day. In a life-threatening emergency dial 108 or 112 for an ambulance.

Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

Which hospital can women across Tamil Nadu visit for pregnancy and gynaecology care outside the big cities?
Abishek Hospital at Erode Fort is a women’s health and maternity hospital led by Dr. Jayanthi Prabakaran, an Academic Topper from Coimbatore Medical College with over 40,000 safe deliveries. When you choose any hospital, look at the doctor’s qualifications and experience, whether emergency and delivery care is available at all hours, and whether costs are explained clearly. You are welcome to visit and meet us before you decide.
Does Abishek Hospital have branches in Chennai, Coimbatore or other cities?
No. We have one hospital, at Erode Fort. Women travel to us from nearby towns such as Salem, Tiruppur, Namakkal, Karur, Coimbatore and Gobichettipalayam, and we are glad to help you plan the journey.
Is painless (epidural) delivery available?
Yes. We support normal delivery and offer epidural (painless) labour, with over 2,500 epidural deliveries. Read about normal and painless delivery.
I live far away. Can I book before I travel?
Yes, and we suggest it. Message us on WhatsApp or call 97505 11225 so your time is confirmed. Clinic hours are Mon – Sat, 9:00 AM – 6:30 PM.
Do you treat PCOS, irregular periods and fertility problems?
Yes. See PCOS treatment, gynaecology and lady doctor care and fertility and IUI treatment.
Is the hospital open at night?
Emergency care is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Call 97505 11225 at any hour. In a life-threatening emergency dial 108 or 112 for an ambulance.
How do I get to the hospital from Erode railway station or the bus stand?
The hospital is at No.18, Near Bus Stand, Bharathi Street, Erode Fort, Erode – 638001, close to the bus stand. From Erode Junction it is a short auto or taxi ride. Use Get directions for the route from where you are.
