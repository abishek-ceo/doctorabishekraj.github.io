About Dr. Jayanthi Prabakaran

Dr. Jayanthi Prabakaran is an Academic Topper from Coimbatore Medical College, one of Tamil Nadu’s well-known medical institutions. Her academic strength and years of dedicated clinical practice have made her a trusted obstetrician and gynaecologist for families in Erode and beyond.

With over 40,000 safe deliveries, more than 7,000 laparoscopic surgeries and over 500 conceptions through IUI, she has made expert women’s healthcare accessible in Erode. She also shares free health education videos in Tamil every day on YouTube (@abishekmedicalcenter), so that every woman can learn from trusted medical knowledge.
