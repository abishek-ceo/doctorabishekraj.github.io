White Discharge in Women: Normal or Not?

வெள்ளைப்படுதல் · எப்போது மருத்துவரைப் பார்க்க வேண்டும்

A little white or clear vaginal discharge is normal and healthy. It is a problem only when it changes in smell, colour or amount, or comes with itching, burning or pain. Here is how to tell the difference.

At a glance

  • Usually normalClear to milky white, little or no smell, changes with the cycle
  • Needs a checkStrong smell, green, yellow or grey colour, itching, pain
  • AvoidWashing inside the vagina and using strong soaps or sprays

What is normal vaginal discharge?

The vagina cleans itself with a small amount of fluid. This discharge is clear or milky white, has little or no smell, and does not itch. It is usually more around ovulation, before a period, during pregnancy and while breastfeeding. Normal discharge does not make the body weak.

What can cause abnormal discharge?

Common causes are:

  • Yeast (fungal) infection: thick, curd-like discharge with itching and burning
  • Bacterial vaginosis: thin, grey-white discharge with a fishy smell
  • Infections passed on by sex, such as trichomonas, which can cause yellow-green, frothy discharge
  • Cervical infection or a polyp on the cervix
  • A forgotten tampon or other object left in the vagina

The treatment is different for each cause, so please do not keep buying creams or tablets without a check-up.

When should I see a doctor?

See a doctor if you have discharge with:

  • a strong or fishy smell
  • green, yellow or grey colour
  • itching, burning, redness or swelling
  • pain in the lower tummy or pain during sex
  • burning while passing urine
  • bleeding between periods or after sex
  • fever
  • a pregnancy: any unusual discharge, or a watery leak, needs a check on the same day

Everyday care

  • Wash only the outside with plain water. Do not wash inside the vagina (douching) and avoid perfumed soaps, sprays and wipes.
  • Wear cotton underwear, change it daily and dry well after a bath.
  • Wipe from front to back after using the toilet.
  • If you are given medicine, finish the full course. Some infections need your partner to be treated too.

Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

Is white discharge before periods normal?
Usually yes. A rise in clear or white discharge before a period is common. See a doctor if it smells, itches, burns or comes with pain.
Does white discharge mean pregnancy?
Not by itself. Discharge can increase in early pregnancy, but a missed period and a pregnancy test are the signs to rely on.
Does white discharge cause weakness or back pain?
Normal discharge does not. If you feel tired, weak or have back pain, ask your doctor to check for other causes, such as low iron (anaemia) or infection.
Can I treat it at home?
Everyday hygiene helps, but the right treatment depends on the cause. Please see a doctor instead of repeating creams or tablets on your own.
