Food and Care in Early Pregnancy

கர்ப்பகால உணவு · முதல் மூன்று மாத பராமரிப்பு

You do not need a special diet. Balanced, home-cooked food, the supplements your doctor prescribes, enough water and rest are the basics. Here is a simple guide.

At a glance

  • Eat balanced mealsDal, vegetables, greens, fruit, milk or curd, grains
  • SupplementsFolic acid, iron and calcium as your doctor prescribes
  • AvoidTobacco, pan, alcohol and raw or undercooked food

What to eat

  • Pulses and dal, eggs, milk and curd, and well-cooked fish or meat for protein and calcium.
  • Green leafy vegetables (keerai), other vegetables and fresh fruit for iron, folate and fibre.
  • Ragi, whole grains, nuts and seeds for energy and minerals.
  • Plenty of water, and clean, freshly prepared food.

What to avoid

  • Tobacco, pan, gutkha and alcohol: no amount is safe.
  • Raw or undercooked meat, fish and eggs, and unpasteurised milk.
  • Unwashed fruit and vegetables, and food left out for hours.
  • Large predatory fish such as shark and swordfish.
  • Too much tea or coffee: keep it to about two cups a day.
  • Unripe (raw) papaya, which is usually advised against in pregnancy.
  • Any herbal, traditional or “tonic” medicine, unless your doctor agrees.

Easing morning sickness

Eat small, frequent meals instead of large ones, keep a dry snack such as a biscuit or puffed rice by the bed, sip water or lemon water through the day, and avoid smells and fried or oily food that make you feel sick. If you cannot keep water down, or you are losing weight, see your doctor, because this may need treatment.

Supplements and check-ups

Your doctor will advise folic acid, iron and calcium, and the dose that suits you. Do not take extra or skip doses without asking. Go for your antenatal visits and tests on time. Read about antenatal care in Erode, and about the early signs of pregnancy.

Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

Can I eat non-vegetarian food in pregnancy?
Yes, if it is fresh and well cooked. Avoid raw or undercooked meat, fish and eggs.
How much tea or coffee is safe?
Keep it to about two cups a day in total.
Should I eat for two?
No. You do not need to double your food, especially in the first trimester. Eat balanced meals and let your doctor guide your weight gain.
