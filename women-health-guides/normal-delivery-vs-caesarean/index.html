Normal Delivery or Caesarean? A Simple Guide

Both are safe when they are the right choice for you and your baby. This guide explains the differences in simple words, and why the decision is best made with your doctor.

At a glance

  • Normal deliveryUsually a shorter stay and quicker recovery
  • CaesareanSurgery, advised for medical reasons
  • Painless labourAn epidural can be offered with a normal delivery

Normal (vaginal) delivery

When pregnancy is healthy and the baby is head down, most women can try a normal delivery. Recovery is usually quicker, the hospital stay is shorter, and there is no abdominal surgery. Risks include tears or a cut (episiotomy) that needs stitches, and sometimes the need for help with forceps or a vacuum, or a change to a caesarean during labour.

Fear of pain is a common reason women ask about a caesarean. An epidural (painless) labour can make a normal delivery much more comfortable.

Caesarean section

A caesarean is an operation to deliver the baby through the tummy. It is planned when, for example, the baby lies feet first or sideways, the placenta covers the cervix (placenta praevia), or a medical condition makes labour unsafe. It becomes an emergency when labour is not progressing, the baby is distressed, or there is heavy bleeding.

Recovery takes longer: a few days in hospital and several weeks at home, with care of the wound and no heavy lifting. Risks include infection, bleeding and blood clots, and it can affect later pregnancies.

Can I have a normal delivery after a caesarean?

Often, yes. It depends on why the earlier caesarean was done, the type of scar on your womb and your current pregnancy. Your doctor will assess you individually and plan the safest way for you to deliver. See our page on high-risk pregnancy care if you have a medical condition.

How to decide

  • Talk to your doctor early, at your antenatal visits, and ask questions.
  • Choose what is safest for you and your baby, rather than a particular date or time.
  • Keep an open mind: a plan for a normal delivery can change if the situation changes.
  • Ask about pain relief for labour.

Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

Is normal delivery always better than a caesarean?
For a healthy pregnancy, a normal delivery usually has a quicker recovery. But a caesarean can be the safest choice, and the best choice is the one your doctor advises for your situation.
Can I choose a caesarean without a medical reason?
Please discuss it with your doctor. Surgery has risks and a longer recovery, so it is usually advised for medical reasons.
Is painless delivery available?
Yes. Epidural (painless) labour is available at Abishek Hospital, with over 2,500 epidural deliveries. Read about normal and painless delivery.
